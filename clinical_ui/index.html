EchoScribe-AI: Multimodal Frame Capture, Vocabulary-Biased Speech Recognition, and LLM-Driven Abdominal Ultrasound Reporting

An on-premises, multimodal clinical documentation platform synchronously integrating hardware ultrasound keyframe capture, domain-biased ASR, LLM structuring benchmarks, and an audited review interface.

Clinical Problem Addressed

Radiologists spend up to 40% of examination time manually typing reports post-exam. Off-the-shelf ASR engines misclassify complex anatomical terminology (e.g. cholelithiasis, hyperechoic lesion), and keyframe images are disconnected from dictation notes.

Mandatory Review Outcomes

  • Title: EchoScribe-AI (Category A Precision)
  • Understanding: Clinical workflow friction & ASR terminology error rate
  • Objectives: Synchronous capture, biased ASR, LLM harness, review UI, clinical taxonomy
  • Scope: In-scope abdominal ultrasound; Out-of-scope automated diagnosis
